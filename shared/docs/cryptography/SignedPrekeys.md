# Signed Prekeys (SPK)

## Purpose
The Signed Prekey guarantees that the cryptographic bundle was actually generated by the owner of the Identity Key, preventing the server from injecting its own keys to MITM the connection.

## Rotation
SPKs are rotated every 7 days by the `RotationScheduler`. When a new SPK is uploaded, the old one is marked `RETIRED`. Retired keys are kept around for a short grace period in case delayed messages arrive encrypted against them.
